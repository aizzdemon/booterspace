Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters at BooterSpace.

This general policy explains what information BooterSpace may collect, how it may be used, and the choices available when you use our jobs, community, reviews, books, messages, and connection features.

Effective date: July 10, 2026 Applies to: BooterSpace website and related services

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use BooterSpace, we may collect information that you provide directly, information created through your activity, and limited technical information from your device or browser.

  • Account information: such as your name, email address, profile photo, login details, and profile preferences.
  • User content: such as job posts, community posts, reviews, messages, comments, saved items, and other information you choose to submit.
  • Usage and device information: such as pages visited, feature interactions, browser type, approximate location derived from technical data, device identifiers, cookies, and similar technologies.
  • Communications: such as support requests, feedback, and notification preferences.

2. How we use information

We use collected information to provide, maintain, personalize, secure, and improve BooterSpace. This may include:

  • Creating and managing user accounts and profiles.
  • Displaying jobs, government opportunities, posts, reviews, books, messages, and connection features.
  • Sending service notifications, account updates, security alerts, and optional communications.
  • Moderating content, preventing abuse, troubleshooting issues, and protecting users and the platform.
  • Analyzing aggregate usage trends to improve performance, design, and features.

3. Cookies and analytics

BooterSpace may use cookies, local storage, analytics tools, and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, understand feature usage, and improve the website. You can usually control cookies through your browser settings, but disabling them may affect some features.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only when needed to operate the service, comply with law, protect rights and safety, or with your direction. Examples include:

  • Service providers that help with hosting, authentication, databases, analytics, notifications, security, or support.
  • Public or semi-public areas where you intentionally post content, such as community posts, reviews, profiles, or listings.
  • Legal, safety, or compliance situations where disclosure is reasonably necessary.
  • Business transfers, such as a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or similar event, subject to appropriate safeguards.

5. Your choices and rights

You may be able to access, update, or delete certain account information through your profile settings. You can also adjust browser cookies, notification permissions, and email preferences where available. Depending on your location, you may have additional privacy rights, such as requesting access, correction, deletion, portability, or restriction of certain processing.

6. Data retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide BooterSpace, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, prevent abuse, and maintain backups. When information is no longer needed, we will delete, anonymize, or securely retain it as appropriate.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information. However, no online service can guarantee complete security, so please use strong passwords and be careful about what you share publicly.

8. Children's privacy

BooterSpace is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

9. Third-party links and services

BooterSpace may link to third-party websites, employers, booksellers, communities, or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and we are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will revise the effective date above and may provide additional notice when appropriate. Continued use of BooterSpace after changes means the updated policy applies.

11. Contact us

If you have questions or requests about this privacy policy, contact BooterSpace at support@booterspace.in.

This is a general privacy policy template for BooterSpace and does not replace legal advice. Consider having the policy reviewed for the exact jurisdictions, services, vendors, and data practices that apply to the project.
