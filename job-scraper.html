How scraping works

The Start button queues a scan for every saved company. The worker checks public career pages and structured JobPosting data. New jobs are sent to admin.html as pending; duplicate/old jobs are skipped.

Scans are processed by GitHub Actions, so the request may take a few minutes to start.
